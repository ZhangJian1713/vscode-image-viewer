The 11 gallery samples other than Blue Marble were resized to a maximum edge of 2000 pixels and re-encoded as JPEG for the demonstration. Fill layout crops the thumbnails in the screenshot. These transformations are permitted by the recorded image licenses.

`gallery-fill-photos.webp`, `gallery-fill-compact-photos.webp`, `gallery-grid-photos.webp`, and `gallery-list-photos.webp` show the same WebView UI in a local browser with a temporary sample-data adapter. Fill crops thumbnails to square cells; Fill + Compact removes thumbnail gaps and permanent captions and shows the density slider adjusted from six to four columns; Grid displays the complete images; the List screenshot is cropped to the file table showing the six Architecture/Art files with their actual demo-file metadata. `set-default-image-editor-command.webp` is a crop of the original command-palette screenshot, focused on the input and command list.

JPEG versions of the six screenshots were compressed with TinyJPG on 2026-10-14 from their original screenshot files, preserving the original pixel dimensions. Attribution and license records remain here and in `SOURCES.json`, which records the resulting dimensions and file sizes.

The READMEs now use WebP images hosted on the user's Tencent Cloud COS image host, produced from the original screenshot files: the single-image screenshot is reduced to 1200 × 636 pixels at quality 74; Fill/Grid screenshots to 1024 × 576 at quality 76; List to 960 × 479 at quality 80; and the default-editor screenshot to 850 × 160 at quality 65. These files use the same source-image credits and licenses. Encoding and resulting file sizes are recorded in `SOURCES.json`. Together with the WebP video poster, the seven images total less than 400,000 bytes; the larger, detailed poster receives more of the size budget.

The local originals were checked against the SHA-256 records in the existing source manifest. For the eight Wikimedia Commons originals, the live imageinfo API confirmed the same licenses and upstream SHA-1 values. Smithsonian object records identify the three selected artwork images as CC0; see their individual source pages and the [Open Access FAQ](https://www.si.edu/openaccess/faq).
